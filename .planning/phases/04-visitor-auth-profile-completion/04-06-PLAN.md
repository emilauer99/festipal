<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| deep link (OS) → app router | Untrusted external href targeting a protected route |
| client → auth (signOut) | Session-invalidation request crosses to better-auth |
| root guard → protected route groups | The authorization decision (which group mounts) |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-4-06-I | Information Disclosure | deep-link content leak while logged out (SC-5) | high | mitigate | Guard decides mounting at the group-layout level (unchanged); the captured destination is only REPLAYED after 'authenticated' is independently reached, never used to bypass the guard (content-leak boundary) |
| T-4-06-E | Elevation of Privilege | deep-link replay bypass | high | mitigate | consume+replay fires strictly on the INTO-authenticated transition; an unauthenticated/`no-profile` state never replays; the href cannot short-circuit the authenticated check |
| T-4-06-T | Tampering | pending-destination durability | low | mitigate | In-memory only (no MMKV/SecureStore) so a stale deep-link intent cannot survive a cold restart into an unrelated session (D-02) |
| T-4-06-D | Denial of Service | splash deadlock on hung resolve | medium | mitigate | Cold-start resolve timeout/fallback routes to Welcome rather than holding the splash indefinitely |
</threat_model>
